Business Confidence Survey 2026

Survey Report

The Business Confidence Survey 2026, conducted by GIC and GCC in September 2026 among GCC members and German companies in Hong Kong, received 63 responses.

Executive summary

  • Competitiveness assessment. Ease of doing business ranks highest at 4.43/5; cost competitiveness is lowest at 2.65/5.
  • Business confidence. Responders' own confidence averages 3.90/5, compared with 3.65/5 for stakeholder confidence.
  • Headcount & talent outlook. 58.7% expect stable headcount; 27.0% find recruiting qualified talent difficult. Salary expectations are the most frequently cited hiring challenge.
  • Middle East impact. 55.6% report a negative impact. Shipping and logistics costs are the most frequently cited affected business area.
  • GCC member satisfaction. Overall membership experience averages 8.2/10, with a Net Promoter Score of +41.

GCC membership questions: 61 responses. Crisis impact areas: 60 responses.

Methodology

The survey received 63 responses in September 2026. Each question uses its non-empty responses as the base; unanswered items are excluded. “Not sure” remains a valid response in the denominator. GCC membership questions received 61 responses; crisis impact areas received 60.

  • Business confidence

    Average ratings use the scale 1 = Very low, 2 = Somewhat low, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Somewhat high, 5 = Very high.

  • Competitiveness

    Average ratings use the scale 1 = Very weak, 2 = Somewhat weak, 3 = Average, 4 = Somewhat strong, 5 = Very strong.

  • Development outlook

    Charts show the percentage expecting improvement, stability or deterioration, together with those who are unsure.

  • GCC membership

    Satisfaction measures use average ratings out of 10. Net Promoter Score uses the recommendation question: the percentage scoring 9–10 minus the percentage scoring 0–6. Scores of 7–8 are passive.

  • Company profile

    Industry shares allow multiple selections. Company size refers to employees in Hong Kong.

Industry, recruitment challenge and crisis impact-area questions allowed multiple selections; percentages can therefore total more than 100%. Mean ratings are arithmetic averages. Displayed percentages are rounded, so totals may differ slightly from 100%. All findings are presented in aggregate form.

GIC Market Entry · 2025–2026

GIC Activities in 2025-2026

During the 2025-2026 reporting year, the Market Entry Services team supported German companies through a wide range of market development activities in Hong Kong and the Greater Bay Area, including delegations, business partner searches, company formations, and office-in-office solutions.

A key focus was on practical market entry solutions. Initiatives such as the Bavarian Store on HKTVmall enabled German producers to test local consumer demand, while tailored delegations from Saxony-Anhalt and Lower Saxony addressed sector-specific opportunities in food, agriculture, logistics and innovation.

Throughout the year, GIC continued to strengthen its role as a long-term partner, helping German companies explore new opportunities, establish business connections and expand their presence in the Hong Kong market.

GIC Market Entry · 2025–2026

Delegations

Highlight9-12 November 2025

Food Delegation from Saxony-Anhalt

In November, we welcomed representatives from government, companies, research institutions, and industry associations in Saxony-Anhalt to explore market opportunities in Hong Kong. The delegation was led by State Secretary Gert Zender from the Ministry of Economic Affairs, Tourism, Agriculture and Forestry of Saxony-Anhalt and organised together with the Saxony-Anhalt Food Network, Agricultural Marketing Society of Saxony-Anhalt, and IHK Halle-Dessau. GIC developed and implemented the Hong Kong programme, which combined political engagement, market briefings, retail and food innovation insights, company visits, networking, and a B2B matchmaking event. Afterwards, the group continued their market exploration journey in Vietnam.

GIC Market Entry · 2025–2026

Promoting Bavarian F&B Brands in Hong Kong

HighlightOctober 2025 – October 2026

Bavarian Store on HKTV Mall

The Bavarian Store on HKTV Mall was developed as a new market entry format for premium Bavarian food brands. The project provides participating companies with visibility on Hong Kong’s largest e-commerce platform and allowed them to validate market demand, increase brand awareness and gain first-hand insights into consumer behaviour in Hong Kong. As such, it provided a low-risk, low-cost tool for companies to collect information before committing to larger investments in the market.

GIC supported the project through participant coordination, communication with local partners, shop implementation support and marketing activities. The project was created in response to feedback from previous market entry activities in cooperation with alp Bayern, including business delegations and online seminars.

GIC Market Entry · 2025–2026

Trade Fairs

GIC serves as the official representative of Spielwarenmesse in Hong Kong.

27-31 January 2026

75th Spielwarenmesse

In January 2026, Spielwarenmesse celebrated its 75th anniversary and once again reaffirmed its position as the world’s leading toy trade fair. The event welcomed 2,313 exhibitors from 68 countries and attracted nearly 60,000 trade visitors from 123 nations. New networking and knowledge-sharing formats such as ToyPitch, LicenseLounge and RedNight provided additional opportunities for exhibitors and visitors to connect, exchange ideas and increase visibility. Emerging product categories, including baby and infant products, as well as the growing “Kidults” segment, generated significant interest and reflected evolving market trends within the global toy industry.

Hong Kong maintained a strong presence at the fair with 175 exhibitors, ranking among the top three exhibitor regions. As the official representative of Spielwarenmesse in Hong Kong, GIC supported participating companies throughout the exhibition process, including space applications, exhibitor coordination, logistics support and pre-show preparation.

25 June 2026

Spark AI Productivity - Certified Hands-on Workshop

Together with our training partner Adaptig AI, we brought together professionals for our certified hands-on workshop “Spark AI Productivity”, focused on learning by doing.

Heidi Adick, Certified Adaptig Trainer, led this highly practical and engaging session, guiding participants in turning experimentation into structured workflows, reusable prompts and measurable productivity gains. Rather than theory alone, participants worked directly with tools such as Copilot, Lovable, NotebookLM, and more to build outputs they can immediately apply in their day-to-day roles, from data analysis and content creation to reports and presentations.

Across the afternoon, participants tackled key challenges many face today: how to integrate AI consistently into daily work, how to move beyond one-off use cases, and how to identify where AI creates real business value. The result was tangible outputs, clearer workflows and the confidence to keep building.

We thank all partners and participants for contributing to such an interactive session, and MVKM Group for hosting us.

GIC Market Entry · 2025–2026

Special Visits & Highlights

28 May 2026

Economic Dialogue and Invest in Germany Roundtable with Katherina Reiche, Federal Minister for Economic Affairs and Energy

On the occasion of Federal Minister Katherina Reiche’s first visit to China this week, we had the opportunity to attend two high-level economic dialogues in Guangzhou yesterday, held as part of her official programme following engagements in Beijing.

GIC Delegate and Chief Representative in Hong Kong, Lea Etsebeth, participated in both dialogues together with Alexander Steinkuhl, President of GCC. The exchanges brought together senior German and Chinese stakeholders to discuss cooperation opportunities, innovation-driven partnerships and resilient supply chains.

The Invest in Germany Roundtable in the afternoon also included an insightful presentation by Robert Herzner from Germany Trade & Invest, who introduced Germany as an attractive investment destination for Chinese companies and shared current investment trends.

Our thanks to the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy and our AHK Greater China colleagues for the opportunity to represent the German business community in Hong Kong and to contribute to the dialogue with partners in China and Germany.

1 / 1
16 April 2026

Roundtable with Gerd Mueller, Director General of UNIDO

During his visit to the region, we had the pleasure of welcoming Gerd Müller, Director General of the United Nations International Development Organisation (UNIDO) and former German Federal Minister for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ), to an exchange with representatives of the German community in Hong Kong.

The discussion provided an opportunity to share perspectives from Hong Kong on the city’s role as a Special Administrative Region of China, current economic and business conditions, and the implications for German companies active in the region. Participants also highlighted Hong Kong’s continued relevance as a global financial centre, a sourcing hub for consumer products, and an important location for intellectual property and technology activities, supported by its regulatory environment and legal framework.

26 January 2026

Roundtable with Deutsche Bundesbank

We had the pleasure of meeting Dr. Burkhard Balz, Member of the Executive Board of the Deutsche Bundesbank, for an insightful roundtable discussion in Hong Kong together with the GCC Board of Directors, Germany Trade & Invest, and other representatives of the German business community.

As a frequent visitor to the city, Dr. Balz took the opportunity to reconnect and exchange perspectives with our participants on Hong Kong’s role in areas such as monetary policy, digital currencies, and international financial cooperation. Many thanks to Dr. Balz and all attendees for the candid and engaging conversation, and special thanks to KPMG for hosting us.
